every lamp on this coast, photographed and dated

Hello

I kept the Wharf Street light from 1971 until it was automated in 1989, and I have photographed every working lamp on this coast since. This page is where I am putting them, one at a time, as the scanner allows.

Things I am after

  • A photograph of Barrow Rock from the sea. I cannot get a boat out there.
  • The 1923 plans for The Mole. The trust says they are lost; I do not believe it.
  • A clock drive for a fourth-order lamp — there was one going at Swapmeet last week and I was too slow.
