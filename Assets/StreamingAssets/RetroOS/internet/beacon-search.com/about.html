About Beacon

Beacon is a full-text index. A program walks from link to link, fetches every document it can reach, and writes down every word in it. When you search, you are searching that writing-down, not the net itself.

How often it comes back

A page that changes gets looked at more often than one that does not. A page that has not changed in a year is fetched about every six weeks, which is why a site can be gone for a month before Beacon notices.

Keeping the crawler out

Put a file called robots.txt at the root of your host and the crawler will read it before anything else. It is an agreement, not a lock: it works because everybody's crawler honours it.

Beacon has no advertising, no directory of its own, and no opinion about what you are looking for. For an opinion, see Kingfisher.
